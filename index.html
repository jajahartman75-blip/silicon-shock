Order lead time vs. cycle time

Cycle time is how long a wafer takes to make. Order lead time is how far ahead TSMC capacity must be booked. For AI-crowded nodes, booking is the long pole.

    Who can make volume like TSMC?

    Nobody fully can. TSMC took about 72% of global foundry revenue in Q2 2026, more than ten times the next player [3]. The practical plan is to split by node: keep leading-edge parts with TSMC but add a non-Taiwan TSMC fab, and dual-source mature parts outside Taiwan.
